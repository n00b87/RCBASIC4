#title SetActorContactStiffnessAndDamping [RCBasic Doc]
#header Sub SetActorContactStiffnessAndDamping( actor, stiffness, damping )

Sets an actor's stiffness and damping values

#list ul	
#li Stiffness: Acts on the positional level to determine how much resistive force is generated per unit of penetration depth. Higher values mean harder, less compressible collisions.
#li Damping: Acts on the velocity level to dissipate relative motion between colliding objects, bringing bouncing or vibrating contacts to rest.
#/list
